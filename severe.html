Strong wind gusts may affect parts of the Metroplex later this evening. Isolated damaging gusts cannot be ruled out.
A cluster of storms that formed in southern OK continues to push south/southeast across the Red River, with the stronger storm moving through Gainesville TX. Other cells are also developing along the outflow, extending into northern Wise and Jack counties. Meanwhile, an older outflow boundary is pushing north and extends from Dallas to Hood counties. This area has become relatively hot today, with upper 90s to near 100 F temperatures. Surface analysis also indicates a low between the storms to the north and northward-moving outflow to the south. Given the steep low level lapse rates ahead of these storms (which are substantial in height), and possibility of increased surface convergence/colliding outflows, it seems possible at least strong outflow will affect the Metroplex later this evening, with perhaps locally damaging gusts assuming storm cores continue to develop as they approach out of the north.
