The risk for severe wind and hail will increase over the next few hours as storms increase in coverage and traverse a surface boundary over southern KS. A few significant severe wind/hail events may occur with the strongest storms. A Severe Thunderstorm Watch will be considered in the short term.
Thunderstorms are rapidly increasing in coverage and intensity along a surface baroclinic boundary. These storms are expected to propagate eastward along the boundary over the next several hours, where ample buoyancy and vertical wind shear are present (i.e. 3000 J/kg MLCAPE and 50 kts of effective bulk shear, respectively). Supercells and multicells should be the main storm mode, at least initially. However, if storms can merge, then a cold-pool-driven MCS may manifest. Given 8+ C/km mid-level lapse rates present (per the 00Z DDC observed sounding), supercell structures may produce 2+ inch diameter hail, with 75+ mph gusts possible with multicell structures or especially an MCS.
